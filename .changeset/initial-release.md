First release of localflake, a local Snowflake emulator on DuckDB, published as a Docker image (`ghcr.io/samlaycock/localflake`) for linux/amd64 and linux/arm64. It speaks the protocol of Snowflake's official drivers and covers Snowflake SQL, semi-structured data, transactions, stages, `PUT`/`GET` and `COPY INTO`.
